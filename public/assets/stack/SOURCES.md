# Stack icon sources

Icons for the Stack section of the Workout Director case study. Fetched on 1 and 2 November 2026. Each mark is
used only to name the tool next to it, in a list of what the app is built with; nothing here suggests that any owner
endorses or sponsors the app. The marks remain trademarks of their owners (credit lines at the end).

Every item is shown in one colour, as a pair of files: `*-dark-ink.*` on the light theme and `*-light-ink.*` on the
dark theme, at 24 px inside a 40 px tile. No full-colour files are kept. Where a file was recoloured or reduced to one
colour from the owner's artwork, the row says exactly how; nothing was drawn by hand.

Simple Icons files come from the npm package `simple-icons` 16.36.0 (https://simpleicons.org,
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons). The package is released under CC0 1.0 (its `LICENSE.md` is the CC0 1.0
Universal text). Its `DISCLAIMER.md` says CC0 covers the package and not necessarily each brand, and asks users to
check each brand's guidelines, so the owner's terms are given for every item below. The Simple Icons files keep the
path exactly and only set the fill: `#090a0b` (the site's text colour) for dark ink and `#f1f6f3` for light ink.

## Owner's terms that this use does not fully meet

The owner (Ashish Kharche) asked for real logos for every item on 2 November 2026. These are the points where an
owner's published terms ask for something this list does not do; they are recorded so the choice can be reviewed.

- Swift: the guidelines ask for at least 30 px on screen, one Swift logo per layout and the publisher's identity on the
  same layout. Here it is 24 px; the site name is on every page.
- SwiftUI and SwiftData: Apple offers these icons on developer.apple.com to identify its own frameworks, not for
  third-party use, and Apple's trademark guidelines do not allow its icons in third-party material without a licence.
  They are also reduced to one colour here.
- AWS Lambda, DynamoDB and AWS CDK: AWS allows its Architecture Icons for architecture diagrams and similar technical
  material, and its icon guidance asks not to crop them or change their colours. Here the white glyph is shown on its
  own in the site's ink, without the coloured square.
- Postgres: the PostgreSQL Trademark Policy says the marks should not be presented with other trademarks or logos and
  should not be modified. Here the single-colour Slonik sits in a list with other marks.
